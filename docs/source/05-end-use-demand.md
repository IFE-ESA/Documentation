# End-use Demand

The end-use sectors of IFE-TIMES-Norway are divided into three parts: Industry, Buildings and Transport, each with a base file that must be included in all analyses (VT_Norway_Industry, VT_Norway_Buildings, and VT_Norway_Transport). These files include the energy service demand, sector-specific commodities, and sector-specific technologies. This chapter describes this data per sector.

IFE-TIMES-Norway intends to include all Norwegian energy consumption and CO{sub}`2` emissions from the use of energy. The division of data in these three sectors is mainly based on the classification of the Norwegian energy balance {cite}`61` and of the emission account {cite}`96`, but with some exceptions. The sectors are divided like this:

Industry: Manufacturing industry, mineral mining, refineries, oil & gas extraction (O&G), construction, agriculture, forestry, fishing (excluding fishing vessels), and data centres

Buildings: Residential sector (private households) and non-residential/commercial buildings (tertiary sector/private and public services) excluding data centres

Transport: By road, sea (including fishing vessels), air, rail and other mobile energy use in manufacturing and services (e.g. defence)

This is further described in each sub-chapter below, and more details are described in {cite}`87`.
